Facilities & diagnostics

A subsonic wind tunnel and a shock tube generate the crossflow conditions needed for liquid-atomization experiments. A combustion test rig supports combustion-noise studies, and a membrane distillation rig supports desalination research.

Measurements span macro to micro scales with particle image velocimetry (PIV), single- and double-view digital holography, and high-speed imaging, complemented by CFD (OpenFOAM) and Cantera-based combustion modeling.
